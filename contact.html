The Landgraf Lab is winding down

The lab will close on 31 March 2027. After a decade of shared work, we want to thank everyone who was part of it — collaborators, students, colleagues and friends — for the ideas, the fieldwork, the long nights and the good company. It was a remarkable ten years. These pages will stay online as an archive of that work.
